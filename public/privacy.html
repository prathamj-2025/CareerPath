Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026. This explains what CareerPath collects, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name and email, plus your school, major and graduation year. If you sign in with Google, Google gives us your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Your learning. Your chosen roadmap and the roadmaps you left, the lessons you read, your assessment scores, stars, module exam results and points.
  • Resume summary (optional). If you upload a resume, it is read in your browser. We save only a short summary with your profile: the file name, its length, and the skills and job titles found in it. The full text is not saved.
  • Placement result. The outcome of the five role questions and the match score for each roadmap.
  • Messages to the study assistant. The questions you type and any lesson text you select.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, save your progress and show it back to you on any device.
  • To recommend a roadmap and show your match scores.
  • To show your school leaderboard.
  • To answer study assistant questions and give feedback on written answers.
  • To fix problems and keep the service secure.

The leaderboard

Students at your school can see your first name, last initial and points. You can switch this off in Profile and you will be hidden from the board, with your progress kept.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. Your account and progress are stored there.
  • Vercel hosts the website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and request time.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google, and Google Gemini is our AI provider. Study assistant questions, the lesson text you are reading, written answers sent for feedback, and (when roadmaps are ranked) your resume text are sent to the AI provider to produce a reply. We do not keep those messages. Google's own terms apply to that processing.

Cookies and tracking

CareerPath stores a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in, and a few settings such as light or dark mode. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell or share your information with advertisers.

Your choices

  • Edit your name, school, major and graduation year in Profile.
  • Hide yourself from the leaderboard in Profile.
  • Delete your account in Profile. This permanently removes your account and saved progress.
  • Withdraw Google access at any time in your Google Account settings under third-party connections.
  • Ask us about your data using the address below.

Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules in the database, so each student can read only their own records. We keep your data while your account exists and remove it when you delete the account. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

CareerPath is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
