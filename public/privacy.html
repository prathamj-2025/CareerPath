Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

CareerPath is a student prototype built for the MOR 531 course at the University of Southern California. It is not a commercial service. It has no database. Your account and progress are saved only in your own browser, on your own device, and never sent to a server.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you create an account with email and password, we use the name, email, school, major and graduation year you type in.
  • Learning activity. The modules you read, your quiz and assessment answers, your scores and stars, and any weekly challenge answers.
  • Resume (optional). If you upload a resume during onboarding, its text is read in your browser to suggest a roadmap.
  • Questions to the study assistant. The questions you type and any text you select from a lesson.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, show your progress, and place you on your school's leaderboard during your visit.
  • To recommend a roadmap from your resume and answers.
  • To answer study assistant questions and give feedback on challenge answers.

Where it goes

  • Your browser. Your account and progress are saved in your browser's local storage so you can sign out and come back on the same device. Nothing is copied to our servers, and other browsers or devices cannot see it. Clearing your site data erases it.
  • Google sign-in. The sign-in token from Google is sent to our server once so it can be checked with Google. Our server returns your name, email and picture to the page and does not save them.
  • AI provider. Study assistant questions, the lesson text you are reading, challenge answers, and (when a roadmap is ranked) your resume text are sent to our AI provider, Google Gemini, to generate a reply. We do not store them. Google's own terms apply to that processing.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and request time.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your information with advertisers.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking cookies.
  • The browser keeps your account and progress (and whether you docked the study assistant panel). Nothing else is stored.

Your choices

  • You can use CareerPath without uploading a resume.
  • You can remove CareerPath's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account settings, under Security, then third-party connections.
  • Because the data lives only in your browser, you can delete all of it by clearing site data for this site. We hold no copy.
