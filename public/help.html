Help and support

Getting started, fixing common problems, and how to reach a person.

Getting started

  1. Create your account with your email and a password, or use Continue with Google. If you use email, open the verification link we send you before signing in.
  2. Tell us a little about you: your school, major and graduation year. Your school sets which course leaderboard you appear on.
  3. Choose a role. Pick one, or answer the five questions to get a match score for each.
  4. Open a module, read a lesson, and take the assessment when you are ready.

Fixing common problems

I cannot sign in

Check the email and password for typos. If you created the account with Google, use Continue with Google. If you signed up with email, make sure you opened the verification link first. You can reset your password from the sign-in page.

The verification or reset email never arrived

Look in spam or junk, and search for CareerPath. Use the Resend button on the sign-in page. Emails can take a few minutes. If nothing arrives, contact us with the email address you used.

My progress looks wrong

Progress is saved to your account, so sign out and back in to refresh it. If a score or star is still missing, send us the sub-module name and what you expected to see.

My resume was rejected

CareerPath checks that the file is a resume. Upload a PDF, Word (.docx) or text resume with your experience, education and skills. Scanned images have no readable text, so export a text PDF instead. You can also continue without a resume.

The study assistant is not answering

The assistant can be unavailable for a short time when its provider is busy. Wait a minute and try again. If it keeps failing, tell us.

Something looks broken on my screen

Reload the page, and try a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. If it is still wrong, send a screenshot and the browser you use.

Still stuck?

Email us at careerpath531@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened, and include a screenshot if you can. Please do not send your password.
