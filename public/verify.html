Verify a certificate

Enter the ID printed on a CareerPath certificate to see who it was issued to and for what.

A certificate is issued only when a student passes CareerPath’s scored assessments, and the result is recorded by the site itself. This page shows the holder’s name, what the certificate is for, the school, the date and the score, and nothing else. If the name or title here does not match the certificate you were given, treat the certificate as not genuine.
