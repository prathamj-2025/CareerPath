About CareerPath

A guided path from where you are to the job you want, with assessments that show you are ready.

What we do

Most career advice says what to learn without saying whether you have learned it. CareerPath starts from a target role, lays out the skills that role needs, teaches each one through that role's lens, and then tests it. Stars are earned only by passing an assessment, so what you see on your roadmap is what you can actually do.

How it works

  1. Pick a role. Choose Product Manager, Project Manager or Business Analyst, or answer five short questions and get a match score for each.
  2. Learn the skills. Each roadmap is split into main modules and sub-modules with short lessons, worked scenarios and practice with hints.
  3. Prove it. Take the assessment to earn one to five stars. Pass a module exam to earn that module's certificate.
  4. Keep going. Join the weekly challenge, climb the leaderboards, and ask the study assistant when something is unclear.

What makes it different

  • Role-specific. The same skill, such as stakeholder management, is taught and tested differently for a product manager than for a project manager.
  • Verified, not self-reported. There is no checkbox for "I know this".
  • Built for students. Free to use, with a leaderboard limited to your own school.

Who built it

CareerPath started as a team project for MOR 531 at the University of Southern California and is now being built as a real product by the same students. Questions, feedback and ideas are welcome on the contact page.
