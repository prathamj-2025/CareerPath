Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the things people ask most. If yours is not here, tell us.

What is CareerPath?

CareerPath is a learning site for students preparing for a specific job. You choose a target role, follow the roadmap of skills that role needs, learn each one through short lessons, and prove it with assessments. Progress moves only through assessment, so nothing can be checked off by hand.

Is it free?

Yes. CareerPath is free to use today.

Which roles are there?

Product Manager, Project Manager and Business Analyst. Each has its own roadmap, lessons and assessments, because the same skill is used differently in each job. The Business Analyst roadmap is being expanded.

How do I choose a role?

If you already know, pick it. If not, answer five short questions with no wrong answers, optionally with your resume, and CareerPath gives each roadmap a match score with a reason. The score is advice, not a rule. You can switch roadmaps at any time from the role menu at the top of the page.

What happens to my progress if I switch roles?

Nothing is lost. Stars stay attached to the roadmap they were earned on, and you can come back to it later. Stars do not transfer between roadmaps, because the same skill is assessed differently for each role.

How do stars work?

Each sub-module has an assessment. Your best score sets your level: 50% earns 1 star, 65% earns 2, 75% earns 3, 85% earns 4, and 95% plus a submitted project earns 5. Three stars means job-ready.

How do I earn a certificate?

Certificates are earned per main module. Get every required sub-module in that module to job-ready (three stars), then pass the module exam with 75% or more.

Do I have to follow the order?

No. The numbering is a suggested order. You can open any sub-module at any time.

Who can see me on the leaderboard?

Students from your own school see your first name, last initial and points. You can turn this off in Profile, and you will be hidden from the board while keeping your progress.

Is the study assistant always right?

No. It is an AI model and it can make mistakes. Use it to understand what you are reading, and check anything important against the lesson or another source.

What happens to my resume?

It is read in your browser. Only a short summary is saved with your profile: the file name, its length, and the skills and role titles found in it. The full text is not stored. If you ask CareerPath to rank roadmaps, the text is sent to our AI provider to produce the ranking and is not kept.

I did not get the verification email.

Check your spam folder first, then use the Resend button on the sign-in page. If it still does not arrive in a few minutes, contact us and we will help.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page choose Forgot password. We will email you a link to set a new one. If you signed up with Google, use Continue with Google instead.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and saved progress. It cannot be undone.

Which browsers work?

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, on a laptop, tablet or phone.
